Description

Apposition phrase שַׂ֤ר וְשֹׁפֵט֙ in Ex. 2:14 forms a combination of two words sounding: śar wəšōpēṭ where the beginning sounds are the same (form of hendiadys). Where is such combination also found?

Lexeme:

שׁפט = CPV[ (participle)

Some other biblical hendiadys:

  • dust and ashes (עָפָר and אֵפֶר)
  • Void and empty - תֹ֨הוּ֙ וָבֹ֔הוּ (Gen 1:2)
  • The wandering fugative - נָ֥ע וָנָ֖ד (Gen 4:12, 14)
